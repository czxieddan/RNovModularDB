## Preamble

The Aperip Heimdall Commons License ("AHCL") is intended to ensure that the software community continuously receives the Complete Corresponding Source, Complete Modification History, dependency relationships, referenced-material relationships, and license materials for versions that are actually used, modified, built, deployed, released, or made available as services over a network.

AHCL permits use, study, reproduction, modification, distribution, and network operation, but those rights are conditioned on continuous compliance with this License's requirements concerning source disclosure, history preservation, dependency transparency, migration to later versions, and downstream propagation. Internal use, test environments, pre-release environments, private networks, and the absence of a formal release do not justify concealing source code or modification history.

AHCL permits mixed-license projects. Its requirements apply to the works and Components within the Covered Scope, including Components that have an Essential Functional Dependency on an AHCL work, rather than automatically to every part of the surrounding project. Each combination must permit simultaneous compliance with the licenses governing its respective parts.

# Attachment B - Minimum Project Notice Layout

Each Covered Scope must have an Applicable `LICENSE` in the repository root or, where AHCL applies to a Component rather than the whole project, in that Component's root directory. Several Covered Scopes may use a centralized layout, or each may have its own complete layout, in accordance with Section 1.12. The directory containing the Applicable `LICENSE` is the base for the paths below. Exactly one of `AHCL/`, `licenses/AHCL/`, `.AHCL/`, or `.ahcl/` must be selected relative to that base as the AHCL Materials Directory. In this Attachment, `[D]` means the selected directory without its trailing slash. The complete operative set for a Covered Scope must remain together in that directory. The minimum layout is:

1. `LICENSE`: identifies the project or Component and the portions governed by AHCL 1.2, lists the Official Publication Location at <https://ahcl.aperip.com>, identifies the selected AHCL Materials Directory, and points to `[D]/AHCL-1.2.md`; it may also list channels for obtaining Special Authorizations as permitted by Section 2.4. If it contains other licensing content, the complete AHCL notice must use the paired markers in Attachment C.
2. `[D]/AHCL-1.2.md`: a complete, unmodified copy of the Official AHCL Text for AHCL 1.2 or a Recognized AHCL Copy; it must not be replaced by the Applicable `LICENSE` file or by an unrecognized translation.
3. `[D]/AHCL-PROJECT-NOTICE.md`: identifies the project, the relevant Covered Scope, the Applicable `LICENSE`, canonical repository, AHCL origin, applicable version, beginning of the Continuous AHCL Licensing Segment, the Effective Date of that scope's first AHCL adoption or of that segment's beginning, and the location of version notices. It must identify the right holders for that scope, except that a Contribution Fork must preserve the original project's right-holder notices without adding a copyright-holder claim prohibited by Section 12.5(b); authorship of contributions may instead remain identified in the Complete Modification History.
4. `[D]/AHCL-VERSION-ADOPTION.md`: contains all Version Adoption Events and supporting records for the relevant Covered Scope; if no Version Adoption Event has occurred for that scope, it must expressly state "None." A centralized file must distinguish the records for each scope.
5. `[D]/AHCL-SOURCE.md`: provides the means of obtaining the public Source Code, Complete Modification History, build materials, and release mappings for the relevant Covered Scope.
6. `[D]/AHCL-DEPENDENCIES.md`: contains the Human-Readable List of Dependencies and Referenced Materials for the relevant Covered Scope and a one-to-one mapping to complete copies of third-party licenses; third-party license copies may be kept together in `[D]/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES/`.
